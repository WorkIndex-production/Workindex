Human-in-the-Loop & Governance

Verification Rules & DPDP Compliance

While AI speeds up mechanical execution by over 70%, professional accountability remains paramount:

  • Human Review Mandatory: Every AI-generated voucher, tax calculation, or notice reply draft must be validated by a qualified Chartered Accountant before filing.
  • DPDP Act Compliance: Client financial data must be encrypted, hosted locally in Indian servers, and excluded from global AI model retraining.
  • Audit Trail Maintenance: Systems maintain immutable log records showing which voucher was parsed by AI and approved by the reviewer for ICAI peer-review readiness.
Common Implementation Pitfalls

What to Avoid When Adopting AI in Tax Work

  • Relying on Generic Foreign AI: Global AI models often fail on Indian TDS slabs, Section 194J vs 194C distinctions, or GST RCM rules. Always use India-tax native solutions.
  • Skipping Bank Reconciliation Verification: Bank statement OCR can misinterpret blurred figures; multi-pass validation rules must be enforced.
  • Ignoring UDIN Requirements: Certificates and audit reports created with AI assistance still require mandatory UDIN generation on the official ICAI portal.
